Criminal Justice Reform

The Problem

The United States holds less than 5% of the world's population but houses nearly 25% of the world's prisoners. Mass incarceration has destroyed families, wasted taxpayer dollars, and failed to reduce crime. The recidivism rate exceeds 75% in many states because we prioritize punishment over rehabilitation.

2.3 million Americans are currently incarcerated — the highest rate in the world, even higher than countries like China, Russia, and Iran.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • End mandatory minimums for non-violent offenses. Judges must have discretion to match punishment to circumstance.
  • Expunge records automatically for non-violent misdemeanors after 5 years of clean conduct. No filing fees. No lawyer required.
  • Restore voting rights immediately upon release from incarceration. Citizenship does not end at the prison gate.
  • Fund rehabilitation — job training, addiction treatment, mental health counseling — at 2x current levels. Shift funding from prisons to people.
  • Ban the box federally for all government contractors and incentivize private employers to do the same through tax credits.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Executive Action
Direct DOJ to deprioritize non-violent drug prosecutions. Issue pardons for federal non-violent drug offenders. Restore voting rights via executive order.
Year 2 — Legislative Push
Pass comprehensive criminal justice reform bill. End mandatory minimums. Fund rehabilitation programs. Automatic expungement system online.
Year 3 — Systemic Change
Close 25% of for-profit prisons. Redirect $10B annually to rehabilitation and reentry programs. National ban-the-box standard in place.
Year 4 — Measurement & Expansion
Publish recidivism data by state. Expand successful programs. Target: Reduce federal prison population by 50% from 2028 baseline.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Reduce federal incarceration rate by 50% within 4 years
  • Cut recidivism to below 30% through rehabilitation funding
  • Restore voting rights to 5+ million formerly incarcerated citizens
  • Save taxpayers $15 billion annually by shifting from prisons to prevention

Economic Opportunity

The Problem

Despite record stock market highs, 60% of Americans live paycheck to paycheck. Wages have stagnated for 40 years while CEO pay has skyrocketed 1,000%. Small businesses — the backbone of American employment — face crushing regulatory and tax burdens. Wealth inequality is at levels not seen since the Great Depression.

$7.25/hour is the federal minimum wage — unchanged since 2009. Adjusted for inflation, it has lost 30% of its purchasing power.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • Raise federal minimum wage to $15/hour, indexed to inflation. No worker working full-time should live in poverty.
  • Small Business Opportunity Fund — $50B in low-interest loans and grants for businesses in underserved communities, with priority for returning citizens and minority entrepreneurs.
  • Financial literacy mandate in all public high schools. Teach budgeting, credit, investing, and entrepreneurship before graduation.
  • Wealth-building accounts for every child born in America — $1,000 seed investment at birth, with matching contributions for low-income families, accessible at age 18 for education, home, or business.
  • Gig economy protections — portable benefits, collective bargaining rights, and minimum earnings guarantees for independent contractors.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Immediate Relief
Raise minimum wage to $12/hour immediately. Launch Small Business Opportunity Fund. Expand EITC for childless workers.
Year 2 — Full Wage & Benefits
Minimum wage reaches $15/hour. Portable benefits pilot for gig workers. Financial literacy curriculum deployed to 50% of schools.
Year 3 — Wealth Building
Universal child wealth accounts launched. Small Business Fund reaches $25B deployed. Gig worker protections law passed.
Year 4 — Measurement & Scale
Poverty rate target: below 8%. Median wage growth target: 5% annually. Small business formation target: 500,000 new businesses/year.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Lift 4 million Americans out of poverty through wage increases
  • Create 500,000 new small businesses annually in underserved communities
  • Reduce wealth gap between Black and white households by 25%
  • Achieve 5% median wage growth annually — double the 40-year average

Education & Youth

The Problem

Student debt exceeds $1.7 trillion, crushing an entire generation. Vocational training is stigmatized despite 6 million unfilled skilled trade jobs. The school-to-prison pipeline funnels disadvantaged youth into incarceration instead of opportunity. Teachers are underpaid and overworked, with 44% leaving within 5 years.

$1.7 trillion in student loan debt. The average graduate owes $37,000. For Black graduates, the average is $52,000.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • Free community college nationwide for all Americans. Two years of tuition, books, and fees covered by federal investment.
  • Vocational training revolution — expand trade schools, apprenticeships, and certificate programs. Partner with unions and employers for guaranteed placement.
  • End the school-to-prison pipeline — eliminate zero-tolerance policies. Replace school resource officers with counselors and social workers at 3:1 ratio.
  • Teacher pay raise — minimum $60,000 starting salary nationwide, with performance bonuses. Loan forgiveness for teachers in underserved areas.
  • Mentorship mandate — every at-risk youth paired with a trained mentor from age 12 through graduation. Funded at $2B annually.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Free Community College
Launch federal community college grant program. Cover 100% of tuition for families earning under $75K. Partial coverage up to $125K.
Year 2 — Vocational Expansion
Open 500 new vocational training centers. Partner with 1,000 employers for apprenticeship programs. Teacher minimum salary: $60K.
Year 3 — Pipeline Reform
Zero-tolerance policies banned in federal-funded schools. Counselor-to-student ratio: 1:250. Mentorship program reaches 1 million youth.
Year 4 — Debt Relief & Scale
Forgive $10K in federal student loans for all borrowers. Forgive full amount for public service workers. 80% high school graduation rate target.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Enroll 5 million students in free community college annually
  • Place 1 million apprentices in skilled trade jobs
  • Reduce school suspensions by 60% through counselor expansion
  • Cut student debt burden by $400 billion through forgiveness and free college

Healthcare Access

The Problem

30 million Americans remain uninsured. Mental health treatment is inaccessible for 60% of those who need it. The opioid epidemic claims 100,000 lives annually. Rural hospitals are closing at a rate of one per month, leaving communities without emergency care.

100,000+ Americans die annually from opioid overdose. For every death, 10 more struggle with addiction. Treatment waitlists exceed 6 months in most states.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • Universal healthcare access through a public option available to all Americans, regardless of employment. Premiums capped at 8% of income.
  • Mental health parity — enforce existing law requiring insurers to cover mental health equally. Expand community mental health centers by 10,000 nationwide.
  • Addiction recovery revolution — treat addiction as a health crisis, not a crime. Fund medication-assisted treatment, residential programs, and harm reduction. Decriminalize possession of small amounts.
  • Rural health rescue — fund mobile clinics, telehealth infrastructure, and incentives for doctors to practice in underserved areas. Reopen closed rural hospitals.
  • Preventive care mandate — free annual physicals, screenings, and vaccinations for all. Invest in public health infrastructure to prevent the next pandemic.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Public Option Launch
Open public option enrollment to all uninsured. Expand Medicaid in holdout states via federal incentives. Launch 2,000 mobile clinics.
Year 2 — Mental Health & Addiction
Open 5,000 new mental health centers. Fund 100,000 new addiction treatment beds. Decriminalize possession in federal law. Fentanyl crisis emergency funding: $10B.
Year 3 — Rural & Preventive
Reopen 50 closed rural hospitals. Deploy telehealth to 100% of rural counties. Universal preventive care: free annual physicals, screenings, vaccines.
Year 4 — Measurement & Universal
Uninsured rate target: below 5%. Mental health access: same-day appointments in 90% of counties. Opioid deaths reduced by 50%.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Insure 30 million uninsured Americans through public option
  • Reduce opioid deaths by 50% through treatment and harm reduction
  • Open 10,000 mental health centers in underserved communities
  • Reopen 50 rural hospitals and deploy mobile clinics to 100% of rural counties

Environmental Justice

The Problem

Low-income communities and communities of color bear disproportionate pollution burdens. Asthma rates are 3x higher near industrial sites. Climate change threatens coastal cities and agricultural heartlands. Meanwhile, clean energy jobs are growing 3x faster than the overall economy — but training access is unequal.

3x higher asthma rates in communities near industrial facilities. 70% of hazardous waste sites are within 1 mile of minority neighborhoods.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • Clean Energy Jobs Corps — train and employ 1 million workers in solar, wind, grid modernization, and energy efficiency. Priority hiring for displaced fossil fuel workers and returning citizens.
  • Pollution reduction mandate — 50% cut in industrial emissions by 2032. Strict enforcement in vulnerable communities. Real-time air quality monitoring public dashboard.
  • Green infrastructure — retrofit 10 million homes for energy efficiency. Install solar on all federal buildings. Build 500 miles of urban green corridors.
  • Climate resilience — fund coastal protection, drought-resistant agriculture, and flood mitigation. Prioritize vulnerable communities in disaster preparedness.
  • Environmental justice enforcement — create a federal Environmental Justice Division with subpoena power. Mandatory community impact assessments for all industrial permits.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Jobs Corps Launch
Open 100 clean energy training centers. Hire 100,000 workers. Launch federal building solar program. Begin industrial emissions monitoring.
Year 2 — Infrastructure & Enforcement
Retrofit 2 million homes. Open Environmental Justice Division. Mandate community impact assessments. Deploy 10,000 air quality monitors.
Year 3 — Scale & Resilience
500,000 clean energy workers employed. 25% emissions reduction achieved. Coastal protection projects begin. Urban green corridors: 200 miles.
Year 4 — Measurement & 2032 Goals
Target: 1 million clean energy jobs. 50% emissions reduction. 100% federal building solar. Zero new hazardous waste sites in vulnerable communities.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Create 1 million clean energy jobs by 2032
  • Reduce industrial emissions by 50% in vulnerable communities
  • Retrofit 10 million homes for energy efficiency
  • Cut asthma rates in high-pollution areas by 40%

Unity & Healing

The Problem

Political polarization is at Civil War-era levels. Trust in institutions — government, media, business, religion — has collapsed. 70% of Americans believe the country is on the wrong track. Social media algorithms amplify division. We have forgotten how to disagree without demonizing.

70% of Americans believe the country is headed in the wrong direction. Trust in government is at 20% — the lowest in recorded history.

The Solution — Five Core Actions

  • National Service Program — 1 year of paid service for all Americans aged 18-22. Options: military, conservation corps, teaching, healthcare, infrastructure. Builds shared purpose and bridges divides.
  • Civic dialogue mandate — fund 10,000 community dialogue circles nationwide. Bring together people who disagree for structured, facilitated conversations. Proven to reduce polarization.
  • Institutional transparency — real-time federal spending dashboard. Lobbyist meeting logs published within 24 hours. End dark money in politics through constitutional amendment push.
  • Media literacy education — mandatory curriculum in all schools. Teach critical thinking, source verification, and recognition of manipulation tactics. Fund independent local journalism.
  • Healing commissions — truth and reconciliation processes for historical injustices: slavery, indigenous displacement, Japanese internment, mass incarceration. Acknowledge the past to build the future.

Implementation Timeline

Year 1 — Service & Dialogue
Launch National Service pilot: 50,000 participants. Open 2,000 dialogue circles. Begin institutional transparency portal. Media literacy pilot in 1,000 schools.
Year 2 — Scale & Transparency
National Service: 200,000 participants. Dialogue circles: 5,000. Federal spending portal: 100% real-time. Dark money disclosure bill passed.
Year 3 — Journalism & Healing
Fund 500 independent local newsrooms. Media literacy in 50% of schools. Launch first Healing Commission on mass incarceration. Indigenous reconciliation process begins.
Year 4 — Measurement & Constitutional
Polarization index target: 20% reduction. Trust in government target: 40%. National Service: 500,000 annually. Constitutional amendment for campaign finance reform introduced.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Enroll 500,000 young Americans annually in National Service
  • Reduce political polarization index by 20%
  • Restore trust in government to 40% (from 20%)
  • Fund 500 independent local newsrooms to combat misinformation
